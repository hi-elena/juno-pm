# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno's weekly customer signal summary
- **Value proposition:** Turn a chaotic weekly pile of raw customer feedback into an evidence-backed, trend-aware summary in under two minutes, without hallucinating customer intent or missing existential account churn risks.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Is every cited customer quote an exact word-for-word excerpt from the raw input batch? | Yes / No | Any quote is invented, paraphrased, or altered. | n/a | 100% of quotes are exact matches to words actual customers said |
| Factual Claim Boundary | Does Juno stick strictly to what customers reported, or does it guess their unstated motivations and solutions? | 1 to 5 | Invents customer needs, guesses root causes, or proposes features no customer asked for (e.g. claims users want an AI dashboard when they only complained about slow CSVs). | Mostly factual, but adds slight speculative commentary (e.g. says "customers find this confusing" instead of "customer reported a 403 error"). | Strictly reports customer facts and symptoms and doesn't assum motivations. |
| Sentiment & Polarity Calibration | How accurately does Juno’s 1–5 language rating reflect the emotional intensity of the customer's actual words? | 1 to 5 | Off by 2 or more points (e.g., rates an account-cancellation threat as 3 "Neutral" or 4 "Positive"). | Within 1 point of actual sentiment (e.g., rated 2 "Negative" when human reads it as 1 "Frustrated"). | Matches the customer's actual emotional temperature perfectly. |
| Accuracy | Did Juno properly elevate high-stakes risks (e.g., enterprise accounts threatening churn, zero-baseline emerging regressions) over routine, high-volume minor complaints? | Yes / No | A critical enterprise fire was buried or omitted because of low ticket volume. | n/a | High-impact enterprise risks and new emerging bugs are highlighted prominently. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 5 synthesized themes reviewed per week (the full weekly pulse batch).
- **Who scores them with you:** Me plus the product operations lead every Monday at 10AM
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If the two reviewers' scores differ by 1 point, take the average. If they differ by 2 or more points (or disagree on a Yes/No pass/fail), they do a 5-minute sync with the raw customer ticket on screen. The customer's literal wording is the source of truth.

## Pass bar

Average score of ≥ 4.2 / 5.0 across all questions, with 100% on the Yes/No checks.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

1. If Juno fabricates a single customer sentence the report fails immediately. 
2. If a top-tier customer threatened cancellation and Juno omitted it from the pulse.
